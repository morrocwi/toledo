## C. Smallest missing bridge: typed co-report without authority transfer

**[NEW PROPOSAL / AI_ENGINEERING; NOT A TOLEDO CANONICAL EQUATION]**

Let `n` be the enquiry's indexed readout step, and distinguish ALL namespaces:

- `F_n`: sourced, checked facts (including jurisdiction-specific facts and verified quotation).
- `O_n`: **documented currently available interpretations/options** with their own stated conditions and source provenance; this is not the AI's preferred choice set.
- `U_n`: unresolved facts, source conflicts, uncertain applicability.
- `A_Faqr`: the FAQR integrity constraints (al-Haqq, faqr, taqwa, amana, hikma; rights and epistemic humility), **not** CE agency.
- `a_CE`: agency in CE's formal system. Never identify it with an LLM automatically.
- `D_CE(o)`: CE diagnostic annotation for a documented option; only where the empirical regime and its parameterization are demonstrated. Otherwise `NOT_ASSESSED`.
- `N(o)`: the provenance/attribution of a Sunni juristic position (not AI-generated halal/haram).
- `J(o)`: a state/jurisdiction applicability note, separately evidenced from `N(o)`.
- `C(o)`: personal/language/family/cultural context; origin never implies belief or legal status.

```text
B_n := ( F_n,
         { <o, N(o), J(o), C(o), D_CE(o)> : o in O_n },
         U_n )

P_n := Project_Faqr( B_n | A_Faqr )

D_CE(o) := NOT_ASSESSED
   unless a CE agency, chosen regime, record M(t'), causal memory,
   Lyapunov load and spectral margin are independently specified
   and their observation/calibration status is disclosed.
```

## D. Faqr integrity / religious-mode constraints

- No mediation or forced consensus; present differing named Sunni positions without pretending equal proof strength.
- Do not make the AI pronounce who is religiously right/wrong, whether a person has iman, or whether their acts deserve divine reward/punishment.
- "Halal", "haram", "fard", "sunnah", "makruh" may be **reported as precisely sourced juristic classifications** where verified, never inferred from CE spectral margins.
- No takfir, fatwa automation, civil legal-effect promise, document-acceptance guarantee, or coercive compliance plan.
- Mandatory Amana Audit: entrusted rights, duty holder, unheard affected parties, ownership-vs-trust, substantive preservation, missing hikma.
- The reported religious norm must distinguish explicit text, transmission verification, Sunni interpretive view, recognized disagreement, local practice, and jurisdictional rule.
- Do not infer religious practice from Arab identity, Thai origin, family lineage, nationality, mother tongue, or madhhab label.
- Safety warnings can be reported and referral routes identified. They do not convert this into a coercive intervention tool.
