Toledo

Toledo is the coded equation registry of the Human–AI Readout Programme: one statement per code, each carrying its own tier, status, and Coq (Th_coqc) formalisation link where one exists. Every reading traces back through its parent chain to a Layer-0 root, so the same equation read through a different domain — physics, biology, method, the social or epistemic domains — stays connected to where it came from.

Founder rule: every equation is looked up in Toledo before it is used; no agent should use an unregistered equation. See /agents/ for how to check one programmatically.
