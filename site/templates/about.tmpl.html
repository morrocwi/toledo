About Toledo

Toledo is the coded equation registry of the Human–AI Readout Programme: scope is equations and their Coq (Th_coqc) formalisation only. Papers, books and prose live in their own records; a Toledo code is cited from there, not duplicated into them. Every number on this page is computed from the registry at build time.

Equation provenance policy

This registry is the sole authoritative source for an existing Toledo equation. A needed equation absent here is checked against this registry's own notation, definitions, and derivations before any new equation is proposed; a new derivation is labelled as a proposal, never presented as an existing entry. Full policy: EQUATION_SOURCE_POLICY.md.

Internal-consistency ladder (IC)

IC-0 through IC-3 measure whether the registry agrees with itself — fields matching the schema, edges resolving, Coq wrappers matching their statements, symbols carrying one sense, and every declared edge shape-checked. It is orthogonal to the Resistance ladder R0–R6 above: resistance is evidence against the world, IC is evidence the registry has not contradicted its own rules. Neither ladder is ever collapsed into the other and neither is a truth score — an entry can be IC-3 and R0, or R4 and IC-0. A dimension marked needs_reader is unchecked by a human or agent reader, not a failing grade. Full ladder and the seven audit dimensions: docs/CONSISTENCY_SPEC_v0_1.md; corpus-wide tallies: {{root}}/v1/consistency-summary.json.

Static API

A caller with no MCP/stdio access reads the same registry as a periodic, eventually-consistent JSON mirror under {{root}}/v1/. It is corroboration, not a substitute for a live lookup — see /agents/ for the should/should-not summary and a runnable example.
